# CMS Cardiology Coverage Policy: Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement

## Covered procedure

Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement (TAVR), CPT-33361, is covered only when all of the following requirements are documented:

- **Primary indication:** Severe symptomatic aortic stenosis (ICD-10 I35.0).
- **Symptoms:** Documented New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class II, III, or IV heart failure symptoms.
- **Hemodynamic severity:** Mean aortic gradient >= 40 mmHg or aortic valve area <= 1.0 cm2.
- **Clinical review:** Formal multidisciplinary heart team sign-off.

## Explicit exclusion

TAVR is not covered for asymptomatic stenosis without hemodynamic compromise.
